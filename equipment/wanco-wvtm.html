2007 Wanco WVTM Solar Message Board

Full-size three-line solar-powered portable message board for work zones, events, utility projects, parking control, and public notifications.

The Wanco WVTM is a full-size portable variable message sign designed for high-visibility traffic and site communication. It features a three-line amber LED display, solar charging, manual height adjustment, and 360-degree rotation for flexible placement.
