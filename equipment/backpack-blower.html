RedMax EBZ8560 Commercial Backpack Blower

High-output commercial backpack blower with 1,000 CFM nozzle airflow and 47 Newtons of blowing force.

The RedMax EBZ8560 is a professional-grade backpack blower built for demanding cleanup work. Its 75.6cc Strato-Charged engine, high airflow, large fuel tank, anti-vibration system, and padded harness make it well suited for leaf cleanup, grass clippings, pine needles, parking lots, and other heavy outdoor cleanup tasks.

Fueling requirements

Use only the approved fuel mix

  • The blower uses a 50:1 two-stroke fuel mixture
  • The unit must be returned at the same fuel level documented at checkout unless otherwise agreed
  • Only approved fresh 50:1 premixed fuel may be used
  • Straight gasoline must not be used
  • Incorrect fuel or oil mixture may cause engine damage
  • Refueling charges may apply
  • Customer is responsible for damage caused by improper fueling
Cleaning and damage requirements

Return the blower in serviceable condition

  • Return the blower clean and free of excessive dust, mud, leaves, grass, sticks, pet waste, and other debris
  • Air intake and cooling areas must not be packed with debris
  • Cleaning charges may apply if returned excessively dirty
  • Customer is responsible for damage caused by misuse, impacts, improper fueling, blocked cooling airflow, neglect, abuse, unauthorized repair, or improper transport
  • Tubes, straps, controls, frame, harness, fuel cap, air-filter cover, and accessories must be returned in the condition documented at checkout
  • The unit may not be sub-rented, loaned, or moved to an unapproved location
  • Final rental approval is required
Safety

Important operating precautions

  • Hearing protection is strongly recommended
  • Eye protection is required
  • Do not operate near bystanders, pets, vehicles, open windows, loose gravel, or fragile property
  • Do not direct the air stream toward people or animals
  • Do not operate indoors or in enclosed spaces
  • Do not use for blowing hazardous, hot, flammable, or unknown materials
  • Operator must understand and follow all safety instructions
  • Protective earmuffs may be included upon request
