Tow-vehicle requirements

A properly equipped tow vehicle is required.

Customer pickup is available with an appropriately rated tow vehicle. The tow vehicle must have a compatible pintle hitch, working trailer-light connection, brake controller, and sufficient towing capacity.

Pickup, delivery, and hauling

Rental support beyond the pickup lot.

Delivery and retrieval may be available for an additional charge. Priority One Rentals can also transport the trailer after it is loaded for an additional hauling charge, subject to material type, weight, distance, access, and disposal requirements. Final approval is required before every rental.
