STIHL FS 91 R Commercial Trimmer

Commercial-duty straight-shaft trimmer with STIHL 4-MIX power and a fast-loading Speed-Feed head.

The STIHL FS 91 R is a commercial-grade trimmer built for residential, commercial, roadside, municipal, and property-maintenance work. Its 28.4cc STIHL 4-MIX engine, solid steel driveshaft, commercial-duty gearbox, loop handle, and Speed-Feed cutting head make it suitable for fence lines, banks, ditches, heavy weeds, parking lots, and general landscape maintenance.

Fueling requirements

Use only the approved fuel mix

  • The trimmer uses fresh approved 50:1 two-stroke premixed fuel
  • The unit must be returned at the same fuel level documented at checkout unless otherwise agreed
  • Straight gasoline must not be used
  • Incorrect fuel or oil mixture may cause engine damage
  • Refueling charges may apply
  • Customer is responsible for damage caused by improper fueling
Cleaning and damage requirements

Return the trimmer in serviceable condition

  • Return the trimmer clean and free of excessive grass, weeds, mud, dust, sticks, string, wire, pet waste, and other debris
  • The cutting head, guard, cooling areas, and air intake must not be packed with debris
  • Cleaning charges may apply if returned excessively dirty
  • Customer is responsible for damage caused by misuse, impacts, improper fueling, blocked cooling airflow, neglect, abuse, unauthorized repair, or improper transport
  • Cutting head, shaft, gearbox, guard, handle, throttle controls, fuel cap, harness ring, and accessories must be returned in the condition documented at checkout
  • The unit may not be sub-rented, loaned, or moved to an unapproved location
  • Final rental approval is required
Safety

Important operating precautions

  • Eye protection is required
  • Hearing protection is strongly recommended
  • Long pants and closed-toe footwear are strongly recommended
  • Keep bystanders, pets, vehicles, windows, and fragile property clear
  • Do not use near loose gravel where thrown objects may cause damage or injury
  • Do not operate indoors or in enclosed spaces
  • Do not use damaged or unapproved cutting attachments
  • Do not remove or bypass the guard
  • Operator must understand and follow all safety instructions
  • Disposable safety glasses and earmuffs may be provided upon request
